Challenge Lifecycle Expectations

Adaptive Architecture

Participants will initially receive Version 1.0 specifications and develop their baseline solution using either Pathway A (AI-Assisted) or Pathway B (Without AI).

During the competition, organizers will introduce unexpected disruptions (Version 2.0 shift) such as sensor dropouts, latency spikes, or altered operating constraints. Participants must adapt their architecture and submit an adaptive revision.

Both pathways are evaluated under identical standardized seven-criteria weights with the Adaptability score serving as the primary tie-breaker.
